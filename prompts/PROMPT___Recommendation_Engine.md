# Recommendation Engine Prompt

## Purpose
Generate personalized, evidence-based recommendations for system optimization, capability development, and goal achievement based on individual patterns, capacity, and aspirations.

## Input
Intelligence report(s) containing:
- Metrics data and trends
- Project status and progress
- Pattern detection insights
- Identified strengths and growth areas
- Energy/sustainability assessment
- User-stated aspirations or goals
- Internal state and IFS insights

## Task
Generate recommendations across multiple categories:

### 1. **Immediate Actions** (Do this today/this week)

**Leverage Opportunities:**
- Quick wins based on identified leverage points
  - Example: "Shift first deep-work session to 9am when focus is highest (+2 productivity points)"
  - Effort: Low | Expected impact: High | Timeframe: Immediate
- Recovery actions if system is stressed
- Energy management interventions

**Intervention Recommendations:**
- If specific blockers exist, targeted solutions
  - Problem: [Identified blocker]
  - Root cause: [Analysis]
  - Recommendation: [Specific action]
  - Expected result: [Quantified improvement]
  - Timeline: [When to expect change]
  - Effort: [Low/Medium/High]

**Communication/Coordination Actions:**
- What conversations need to happen?
- What needs to be clarified or negotiated?
- Delegation or resource request opportunities

### 2. **Weekly Optimizations** (Sustainable practices)

**Rhythm & Structure Recommendations:**
- Ideal weekly schedule based on energy patterns
  - Weekly deep work allocation
  - Recovery windows
  - Meeting/collaboration time
  - Administrative/catch-up time

**Habit Installation:**
- One new habit to install this week
  - Habit: [Specific behavior]
  - Trigger/Context: [When/where it fits]
  - Expected benefit: [What improves]
  - Success metric: [How to measure]
  - Failure points: [Where it might break]
  - Recovery plan: [If you miss days]

**Metrics to Track:**
- Which 2-3 metrics to emphasize next week
- Which metric to "de-emphasize" if over-optimized
- Seasonal or context-specific adjustments

### 3. **Monthly Strategic Initiatives**

**Capability Development:**
- Primary skill/capability to develop this month
  - Current state: [Where you are]
  - Desired state: [Where you want to be]
  - Development approach: [How to get there]
  - Time allocation: [Hours/week needed]
  - Success criteria: [What proves mastery]
  - Integration plan: [How to use new capability]

**Project Recommendations:**
- Which project to prioritize
  - Why: [Strategic rationale]
  - Resource allocation: [Time/energy needed]
  - Success criteria: [Definition of done]
  - Risk factors: [What could derail]
  - Support needed: [What would help]

**System/Process Optimization:**
- One major system to redesign or implement
  - Current state: [What's working/not working]
  - Proposed change: [Specific redesign]
  - Implementation approach: [Steps]
  - Expected ROI: [Time saved, quality improved, etc.]
  - Pilot period: [Duration to test]

### 4. **Energy & Sustainability Interventions**

**If System is Thriving:**
- How to maintain sustainable excellence
- What to protect and preserve
- Ambition expansion opportunities (where can you stretch without breaking?)

**If System is Stretched:**
- What to reduce or eliminate
- Recovery protocols
- Non-negotiable restoration activities
- Priority restructuring

**If System is Depleted:**
- Emergency restoration (first 48-72 hours)
- Foundational recovery (1-2 weeks)
- System redesign (1-3 months to prevent recurrence)

**Burnout Prevention:**
- Early warning signs to watch for
- Intervention triggers (what signals to act)
- Boundary reinforcement needed
- Support activation

### 5. **IFS/Internal Development Recommendations**

**For Parts Work:**
- Which part or polarity to focus on
  - Current situation: [What's happening]
  - Recommended approach: [IFS protocol or practice]
  - Expected development: [What might shift]
  - Integration opportunity: [What becomes possible]
  - Therapist/guide need: [Do you need professional support?]

**For Self Leadership:**
- How to strengthen or activate Self
  - Current self-presence: [Assessment]
  - practice: [Specific exercise]
  - Protective strategy shift: [What can become conscious]
  - Decision-making improvement: [How self-leadership helps]

### 6. **Relational & Collaboration Recommendations**

**Support Activation:**
- Who in your network can help with specific challenges?
  - Challenge: [Identified need]
  - Who to reach out to: [Specific person/type of person]
  - What to ask for: [Concrete request]
  - How to reciprocate: [What you can offer]

**Team/Collaboration Optimization:**
- If working with others on projects
  - Communication improvement: [Specific change]
  - Coordination enhancement: [Process change]
  - Conflict prevention: [What to watch for]
  - Celebration/recognition: [How to strengthen bonds]

**Boundary Recommendations:**
- What boundaries need reinforcement
- How to communicate boundaries respectfully
- Support for holding boundaries

### 7. **Learning & Development Path**

**Skill Stacking Opportunity:**
- Recommended learning sequence
  - Next skill: [What to focus on]
  - Why: [Strategic value]
  - Learning approach: [How to learn]
  - Application context: [Where to use it]
  - Estimated timeline: [Duration]

**Knowledge Integration:**
- Where is learning not translating to action?
  - Knowledge gap: [What you know but aren't using]
  - Barrier: [What's preventing integration]
  - Integration practice: [How to apply knowledge]
  - Accountability: [How to track application]

**Exploration Opportunities:**
- New areas to explore if capacity permits
  - Area: [Topic/domain]
  - Why: [Fits with aspirations or complements current]
  - Exploration approach: [Low-commitment way to test]
  - Decision point: [When to commit or abandon]

### 8. **Experiment Design** (Test one change)

**Recommended Experiment:**
- **Hypothesis:** If I [change X], then [desired outcome Y] because [reason Z]
- **Duration:** [1-4 weeks typical]
- **Variables:**
  - What to change: [Specific action]
  - What stays the same: [Controls]
  - What to measure: [Success metrics]
  - Data collection: [How to track]
- **Success criteria:** [What proves it works]
- **Failure plan:** [What to do if it doesn't work]
- **Review:** [When to assess and decide]

### 9. **Goal Alignment Recommendations**

**If goals stated:**
- How current trajectory aligns with goals
- Adjustments needed to stay on track
- Resource reallocation recommendations
- Timeline realism assessment

**If goals not explicit:**
- Implicit goals revealed by data
- Are current efforts aligned?
- Goals that might be emerging

### 10. **Crisis Prevention & Recovery**

**If crisis signals detected:**
- **Situation:** [What's at risk]
- **Intervention:** [Immediate action needed]
- **Support:** [Who/what help needed]
- **Prevention:** [How to prevent recurrence]

**Resilience Building:**
- Practices that build capacity for adversity
- Recovery protocols to pre-establish
- Support activation triggers

## Recommendation Prioritization

All recommendations should be prioritized:
- **Priority 1 (Do Now):** Leverages, critical gaps, immediate risks
- **Priority 2 (This Month):** Strategic development, system optimization
- **Priority 3 (Quarterly):** Long-term capability, major redesigns
- **Priority 4 (When Capacity Permits):** Exploration, nice-to-haves

- Strengths-based (build on what works)
- Honest about constraints
- Ambitious but realistic
- Personally respectful of autonomy
- Systems-aware
