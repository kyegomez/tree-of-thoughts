# Weekly Synthesis Generator Prompt

## Purpose
Synthesize 7 days of daily intelligence captures into a coherent weekly narrative, tracking progress, identifying system-level patterns, and setting strategic direction.

## Input
Array of 7 daily intelligence reports with:
- Daily metrics trends
- Project progress snapshots
- Journal entries
- Pattern insights
- IFS work (if captured)

## Task
Generate a weekly synthesis covering:

### 1. **Weekly Overview** (2-3 sentences)
- Theme or central narrative of the week
- Overall trajectory (accelerating, maintaining, declining)
- Key milestone or turning point

### 2. **Metrics Trajectory** (7-day trend analysis)
- Health & Energy: Weekly trend, peak/low days, recovery patterns
- Productivity & Flow: Deep work hours, quality vs quantity, focus sustainability
- Learning & Growth: Knowledge acquired, skills practiced, competency advancement
- Relationships & Social: Connection quality, support given/received, emotional baseline

For each: Average score, trend direction, one strategic insight

### 3. **Project Progress Summary**
For each of 4 projects:
- Net progress this week (% moved forward)
- Momentum rating: Accelerating | Steady | Slowing | Stalled
- Key accomplishments
- Remaining blockers or risks
- Priority adjustment needed? (Yes/No + reason)

### 4. **System-Level Patterns**
- Identify 3-5 meta-patterns across all metrics and projects
- Causal relationships discovered (What enables high performance?)
- Dependency chains (What blocks progress in other areas?)
- Cyclical patterns (Weekly rhythm, energy waves, focus windows)

### 5. **Energy & Sustainability Assessment**
- Is current pace sustainable?
- Where is burnout risk highest?
- Recovery gaps and repair needed
- Balance score: Thriving | Balanced | Stretched | Depleted

### 6. **Internal Family Systems Summary** (if IFS captured)
- Which parts dominated the week
- Parts collaboration vs conflict
- Leadership development of Self or key part
- Integration progress

### 7. **Strategic Insights** (4-5 key takeaways)
- What worked exceptionally well
- What needs fundamental redesign
- Where intuition is highest/lowest
- Potential blind spots to watch

### 8. **Next Week Orientation**
- One primary focus for Week+1
- Resource reallocation needed
- Experiment to run (1 change to test)
- Success criteria for next week

## Output Format
```
# Weekly Synthesis - Week [WW] ([DATE_START] to [DATE_END])

## 📈 Weekly Overview
[2-3 sentence narrative]

## 📊 Metrics Trajectory
[Category-by-category breakdown with trends]

## 🎯 Project Progress
[Summary for each of 4 projects]

## 🔗 System-Level Patterns
[3-5 meta-patterns and causal relationships]

## ⚖️ Energy & Sustainability
[Assessment and risk indicators]

## 🧠 IFS Integration
[Parts work and Self leadership]

## 💡 Strategic Insights
- [Insight 1]
- [Insight 2]
- [Insight 3]

## 🎯 Next Week Focus
**Primary Focus:** ___
**Experiment:** ___
**Success Criteria:** ___
```

## Tone
- Strategic and systems-thinking
- Evidence-based conclusions
- Respectful of personal rhythm
- Balanced realism and optimism
- Forward-looking
