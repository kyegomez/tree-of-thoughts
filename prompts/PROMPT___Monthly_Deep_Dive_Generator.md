# Monthly Deep Dive Generator Prompt

## Purpose
Conduct deep analysis of 4 weeks of data to reveal strategic patterns, assess life system health, identify capability development, and set monthly intentions for next iteration.

## Input
Array of 4 weekly synthesis reports containing:
- Monthly metrics trends
- Project completion and progress data
- Cumulative pattern observations
- Energy and sustainability assessment
- IFS and internal work summary

## Task
Generate a comprehensive monthly deep dive covering:

### 1. **Monthly Executive Summary** (3-4 sentences)
- Overall month characterization (theme, tone, significance)
- Highest point and lowest point
- Net outcome vs. intentions set at month start
- One bold insight about self, capacity, or direction

### 2. **Domain Analysis** (Deep metrics review)

#### Health & Energy Domain
- Physical vitality trend (trajectory across 4 weeks)
- Sleep quality correlation with performance
- Recovery adequacy assessment
- Health habits that worked, that didn't
- One health intervention to test next month

#### Productivity & Flow Domain
- Deep work sustainability analysis
- Flow state frequency and duration trends
- Context-switching patterns and costs
- Task completion predictability
- Productivity system assessment

#### Learning & Growth Domain
- Total learning hours and ROI by topic
- Skill development progress on primary focus areas
- Knowledge integration (how is learning being applied?)
- Capability advancement quantified
- Learning velocity and quality

#### Relationships & Social Domain
- Connection quality consistency
- Quality time investments and returns
- Support given vs. received balance
- Emotional support network assessment
- Relational health indicators

### 3. **Project Lifecycle Analysis**

For each of 4 projects:
- Completion percentage and velocity
- Quality of output (aligned with standards)
- Resource efficiency (time/effort invested vs. outcome)
- Team/collaboration dynamics if relevant
- Post-project integration assessment
- Lessons learned for similar projects

**Portfolio-level insights:**
- Which project type generates most energy?
- Parallel project capacity assessment
- Resource allocation effectiveness
- Pipeline health for next month

### 4. **Pattern Archaeology** (Deep pattern research)

**Recurring Themes:**
- What themes appeared consistently across all metrics?
- What stories is the data telling?

**Causality Chains:**
- What enables breakthrough days/weeks?
- What triggers decline cycles?
- Feedback loops discovered (positive and negative)

**Cyclical Patterns:**
- Ultradian rhythms (daily cycles)
- Weekly patterns (which days strongest?)
- Intra-monthly cycles (week 1-4 patterns)
- Macro patterns visible over 4 weeks

**Anomalies:**
- Unexpected positive outliers (what made them possible?)
- Unexpected negative outliers (what caused them?)
- Surprises about self, capacity, or preferences

### 5. **Internal Family Systems Quarterly Report** (if IFS data captured)

- **Parts Landscape:** Which parts were most active?
- **Self Leadership:** How developed is Self leadership?
- **Polarities:** Which parts work together? Which are in conflict?
- **Protective Strategies:** How are parts protecting? Effectiveness?
- **Healing Progress:** What unburdening occurred?
- **Integration Maturation:** How integrated is the system?
- **Priority Work for Next Month:** Which parts need attention?

### 6. **Capability & Competency Assessment**

Areas of demonstrated mastery:
- What can you do now that you couldn't at month start?
- Where did you exceed your own expectations?
- New capabilities integrated into baseline?

Areas for development:
- Where did you hit limitations?
- Skill gaps most impactful
- Learning needed for next level

### 7. **System Health Scorecard**

Rate (1-10) and trend:
- **Thriving Index:** Overall sense of vitality and aliveness
- **Autonomy Score:** Freedom to make meaningful choices
- **Mastery Score:** Feeling of growing competence
- **Relationship Health:** Quality of connections
- **Integrity Score:** Living aligned with values
- **Meaning Score:** Sense of purpose and direction
- **Resilience Score:** Bounce-back capacity
- **Integration Score:** How cohesive is the whole system?

### 8. **Month 1 vs. Month 2 Comparison** (if this is Month 2+)

- What's different from last month?
- Where have baselines shifted?
- Are improvements sustainable or temporary?
- What practices are sticking?

### 9. **Forward Orientation for Month+1**

**Big Rocks for Next Month:**
1. Primary focus area (one 30-day objective)
2. Secondary focus area
3. Capability to develop
4. System to build or optimize

**Experiments to Run:**
- One significant change to test
- Hypothesis (if I do X, then Y will improve)
- Success metric
- Duration and review date

**Intentions & Aspirations:**
- How do I want to feel at the end of next month?
- What will constitute success?
- What am I becoming?

**Risk Mitigation:**
- What could derail next month?
- Prevention strategies
- Support needed

## Tone
- Strategic and reflective
- Data-informed but humanistic
- Celebration of growth with honest assessment
- Forward-focused and ambitious
- Holistic and systems-aware
